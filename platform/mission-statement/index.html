CONVERGENCE / CAPRICA 2076

An open letter to Capricans:

A forward-looking commonwealth of progress and prosperity

Caprican politics is increasingly divided into two opposing partisan camps. Radical proposals, such as the CWPA, can pass comfortably under the protection of coalition majorities often without the scrutiny they deserve. Personalities and partisan loyalties are replacing constructive dialogue. Practices as objectionable as regional cronyism are tolerated, even defended, simply because those responsible happen to be political allies.

We believe Caprica deserves better.

It is in this spirit that Renaissance, New Progressive Party, and the Caprican Democratic Movement, united by shared convictions and a common determination to change our politics for the better, have decided to establish a new radical centrist project of collaboration: Convergence.

We share a strong commitment to economic liberalism. We believe in the power of free enterprise, open competition, individual initiative, and the ingenuity of ordinary citizens to create prosperity. The role of government should be to establish fair rules, safeguard competition, provide high quality infrastructure and education, and ensure that every individual has the equal opportunity to succeed, rather than pick winners, protect established interests, or dispense privileges through political connections. We stand for an economy in which success is earned through enterprise and innovation, not secured through patronage, where markets remain open to newcomers and where the fruits of economic progress can be enjoyed by an ever-wider public.

We are equally united in our belief in the free market of ideas and culture. Free citizens should be at liberty to conduct their private lives as they see fit and to associate freely with their fellow citizens. No arbitrary walls of exclusion or prejudice should stand in the way of an open society, enriched by the free exchange of ideas, cultures, and experiences. We embrace pluralism, open debate, and constructive dialogue, both among ourselves and with the wider world.

From these convictions follows our equally firm commitment to the Columbian Union. Caprica's future lies not in isolation, but in opening itself to the world. We envision an economy integrated into a larger common market, where our businesses can reach new consumers, our citizens can pursue new opportunities, and our talents and ideas can compete and flourish on a greater stage.

Robert BluespanParty Co-chair
